# M / Cohesion - Scientific Bridges

## Core question

How can differentiated systems form, maintain, or restore coherence without erasing their internal differences?

## Relevant scientific families

- dynamical systems and attractors;
- control theory and homeostasis;
- developmental bioelectricity and morphological regulation;
- network synchronization and modular integration;
- synergetics and order parameters;
- active matter and collective behavior.

## Guardrail

`M` should not be identified literally with gravity, love, or negative entropy.

The scientific task is to define a measurable coherence operator and identify conditions under which it counterbalances differentiation.

## Candidate criterion

A strong version of M would increase coordination at one scale without destroying useful differentiation at lower scales.
