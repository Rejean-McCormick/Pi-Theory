# Compact Dictionary

**Source** - Ultimate principle in the theory. It may receive a theological reading, but it is not automatically identical to pi.

**Ideal circle** - Exact Euclidean structure with rotational symmetry, equal radii, no privileged angular direction, and a tangent at every circumference point.

**pi_exact** - The exact mathematical invariant `C/d` for Euclidean circles.

**pi_represented** - A positional expansion of pi in a chosen base. Decimal digits belong here.

**First distinction** - The hypothesized transition from undifferentiated unity to structured multiplicity.

**M** - Cohesion, reintegration, or the tendency of differentiated parts to form a coherent whole.

**BIEN** - Orientation toward coherence that preserves rather than captures difference.

**GIHECEF** - Differentiation, processing, transformation.

**JNON** - Integration, assembly, recombination.

**B0UM** - Threshold transition, regime change, or creation event. Big Bang is a candidate instance, not the only definition.

**SIZE** - Magnitude, scale, or entry into a new measurable regime.

**Recursive return** - Repeated movement from differentiation toward integration and a new higher-order whole.

**Residual difference** - A provisional term for the difference that remains after reintegration and may open a new cycle.

**Attunement** - Hypothesis that mathematics, cognition, language, and nature can exhibit convergent structures because they arise under shared deep constraints.

**Exploratory decoding** - A discovery process in which rules were not fixed before results were seen.

**Prospective test** - A future analysis whose rules and evaluation criteria are frozen before inspection.
