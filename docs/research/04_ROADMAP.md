# Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 - Stabilize the object of study

- maintain one canonical definition for each operator;
- keep mathematical facts, decoding observations, interpretations, and hypotheses separate;
- preserve the exact ex-post decoding ledger;
- document the real exploratory discovery history;
- remove legacy claims that no longer survive scrutiny.

## Phase 2 - Comparative structural convergence

This is the first major validation program for the metaphysical architecture.

1. Freeze the abstract Pi template before consulting a comparison source:

```text
unity / source
-> distinction
-> cohesion / orientation
-> differentiation <-> integration
-> threshold / manifestation
-> scale
-> return / recursive reorganization
```

2. Build a primary-source corpus from established philosophy, theology, religious literature, mythology, fiction, and relevant scientific theories.

3. Compare relations, not merely words. A strong match concerns the order and functional relations among concepts, not the presence of isolated terms such as love, good, creation, or infinity.

4. Record strong matches, partial matches, contradictions, and missing elements.

5. Distinguish independent convergence from historical transmission or shared cultural ancestry.

6. Do not modify the Pi template to fit a source after the comparison begins.

Deliverable: a source-audited convergence atlas showing where Pi Theory strongly aligns, partially aligns, or conflicts with established thought.

## Phase 3 - Reconstruct and audit the decoding

- publish the complete stabilized pipeline;
- distinguish discovery heuristics from the final reproducible ledger;
- document every known degree of freedom;
- provide executable code where useful;
- preserve provenance for changes in interpretation.

This phase establishes what the decoding procedure does. It does not by itself validate the metaphysics.

## Phase 4 - Formalize the metaphysical dynamics

- define differentiation, integration, coherence, thresholds, return, and scale transitions more precisely;
- determine which relations are logical, metaphysical, analogical, or potentially measurable;
- identify where the theory makes claims that can contact independent science.

## Phase 5 - Domain-specific scientific contact

Where Pi Theory makes claims about physical reality, use the methods of the relevant field rather than a generic validation checklist.

Priority contact points include:

- dynamical systems and attractors;
- critical transitions and bifurcations;
- scale and renormalization;
- physical memory and path dependence;
- developmental regulation and bioelectricity;
- origin-of-life organization;
- cognition and multi-scale integration.

## Phase 6 - Statistical selectivity of the decoding

Null models, random controls, cross-constant comparisons, and blind evaluation are appropriate here for a narrow question:

> How exceptional is the stabilized decoding under a clearly defined space of alternative procedures and inputs?

They are not a universal test of Pi Theory's metaphysical truth.

## Phase 7 - Language, culture, and attunement

Study whether recurring semantic and phonetic forms are better explained by shared embodiment, cultural inheritance, historical transmission, convergent cognition, or a deeper common structure.

## Phase 8 - Pi-specific consequences

Seek consequences that depend non-trivially on pi rather than on a generic process vocabulary. A quantitative physical prediction would be especially valuable if the theory reaches a sufficiently formal stage, but it is not the only meaningful standard for assessing the metaphysical program.
