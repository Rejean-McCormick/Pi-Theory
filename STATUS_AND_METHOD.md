# Status and Method

Pi Theory is currently a speculative research program, not an established physical theory.

## Established layer

The repository relies on standard mathematics concerning Euclidean circles, pi, irrationality, symmetry, and positional representation.

## Exploratory layer

The early-digit decoding was discovered after experimentation. It is reproducible under its stabilized procedure but was not preregistered.

## Interpretive layer

The mapping from tokens to cohesion, orientation, differentiation, integration, transition, and scale is interpretive.

## Hypothesis layer

Claims about a generative grammar of reality, recursive cosmology, matter as pi-specific memory, linguistic attunement, or the Big Bang as a B0UM event remain hypotheses.

## Research standard

Future evidence should come from frozen procedures, null models, blind evaluation, cross-constant comparisons, and quantitative predictions that can fail.
