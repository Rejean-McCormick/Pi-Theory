# Status and Method

Pi Theory is currently a speculative metaphysical research program with mathematical, interpretive, historical, and potentially scientific layers. It is not an established physical theory.

## No single validation protocol fits every layer

Pi Theory should not be evaluated as if metaphysics, textual interpretation, mathematics, and engineering were the same kind of inquiry.

Different claims require different forms of assessment:

| Claim type | Appropriate assessment |
|---|---|
| Mathematical claim | proof, calculation, exact reproduction |
| Decoding claim | provenance, exact ledger, reproducibility under the stabilized procedure |
| Metaphysical claim | conceptual coherence, explanatory reach, internal consistency, comparative structural convergence |
| Historical or literary convergence | primary-source scholarship, contextual reading, structural comparison, genealogy and independence checks |
| Physical or biological claim | domain-specific empirical evidence and quantitative testing |
| Statistical claim about the decoding | explicit search space, controls, null models, and prospective procedures |

A method is relevant only to the claim it can actually assess. Null models can test whether a decoding pattern is statistically selective under a defined search space. They cannot, by themselves, decide whether a metaphysical structure is true.

## Established layer

The repository relies on standard mathematics concerning Euclidean circles, pi, irrationality, symmetry, and positional representation.

## Exploratory decoding layer

The early-digit decoding was discovered through exploration. It was not preregistered. The current procedure is an ex-post stabilized reconstruction that allows the result to be reproduced.

The correct historical sequence is:

```text
exploration -> structural and linguistic cues -> block discovery -> stabilized ledger -> later testing
```

Legacy texts that describe the original rules as fixed a priori are not authoritative on discovery history.

## Interpretive layer

The mapping from the stabilized tokens to cohesion, orientation, differentiation, integration, transition, and scale is interpretive. The interpretation should be judged first by structural coherence and by comparison with independently established bodies of thought, not by pretending that a metaphysical reading is an engineering measurement.

## Hypothesis layer

Claims about a generative grammar of reality, recursive cosmology, matter as pi-specific memory, linguistic attunement, or the Big Bang as a B0UM event remain hypotheses.

## First comparative validation target

The first major validation program is comparative structural convergence.

The Pi-derived template is frozen first. It is then compared with established philosophy, theology, religious literature, mythology, fiction, and scientific theories that independently describe structures such as unity, differentiation, integration, creation thresholds, return, and scale.

The purpose is not to prove Pi Theory by authority. It is to test whether the structure recovered from pi is idiosyncratic or whether it recurs, with meaningful relational similarity, across historically established attempts to describe reality.

## Research standard

Use the method appropriate to each layer. Preserve mismatches and negative evidence. Do not use historical parallels to rewrite the Pi template after the fact. Do not use statistical tools to answer metaphysical questions they cannot answer. Where Pi Theory makes physical claims, use the standards of the relevant science.
