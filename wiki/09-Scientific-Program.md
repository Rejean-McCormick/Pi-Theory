# Scientific Program

Pi Theory should make contact with science where it makes scientific claims, but scientific validation tools should not be treated as universal judges of metaphysics.

## Match the method to the claim

- Mathematics: proof and exact calculation.
- Decoding: provenance and reproducibility.
- Metaphysics: conceptual coherence and comparative structural convergence.
- Historical claims: primary-source scholarship and context.
- Physical claims: empirical and quantitative testing in the relevant field.
- Statistical claims about the decoding: controls, null models, and prospective procedures.

## Scientific contact points

The most promising independent domains include:

- dynamical systems and attractors;
- bifurcations and critical transitions;
- renormalization and scale;
- physical memory and path dependence;
- developmental regulation and bioelectricity;
- origin-of-life organization;
- active matter and collective order;
- cognition and multi-scale integration.

The objective is not to rename known mechanisms with Pi Theory vocabulary. It is to determine whether the same relational architecture can be formalized and whether it adds explanatory or predictive value.

## Statistical decoding work

Control constants, random sequences, blind ratings, and preregistration remain useful, but for a narrow question: how selective or predictive is the stabilized decoding under a defined search space?

They do not establish or refute the metaphysical ontology by themselves.
