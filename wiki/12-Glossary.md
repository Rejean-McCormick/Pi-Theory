# Glossary

**Source** - deepest principle in the theory; may receive a theological interpretation.

**Ideal circle** - Euclidean structure with exact rotational symmetry and equal radii.

**pi_exact** - exact invariant `C/d`.

**pi_represented** - positional digits used to express pi in a chosen base.

**M** - cohesion or reintegration.

**BIEN** - orientation toward non-capturing coherence.

**GIHECEF** - differentiation or transformation.

**JNON** - integration or assembly.

**B0UM** - threshold transition or creation event.

**SIZE** - scale, magnitude, measurable regime.

**Recursive return** - repeated movement from differentiation toward reintegration and a new whole.

**Residual difference** - provisional remainder that prevents exact repetition and may open a new cycle.

**Attunement** - hypothesis of convergent structures across nature, cognition, and language under shared constraints.

**Exploratory** - discovered with flexibility before rules were frozen.

**Prospective** - specified before inspection of the test data.
