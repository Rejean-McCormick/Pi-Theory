# Sources and Provenance

The current repository contains two conceptual layers:

- `docs/`: canonical working theory;
- `wiki/`: public explanatory layer.

Earlier project documents were generated through AI-assisted synthesis of the author's ideas. They are historically useful but not authoritative.

The public English pack intentionally excludes the full mixed-language binary archive. This avoids presenting obsolete AI-generated statements as current theory and avoids filename and rendering problems.

An English manifest of the legacy source families is available in `provenance/LEGACY_SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`.

Historical quotations or scholarly claims inherited from legacy documents must be independently verified before publication.
