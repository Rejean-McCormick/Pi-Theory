# Inversion, Return, and Cycles

## Why inversion is a problem

The decoding reverses a finite window. No known property of pi currently explains why that reversal should carry ontological meaning.

## Return hypothesis

A possible interpretation is:

```text
manifestation: source -> differentiated world
reflection: differentiated world -> source
```

A conscious observer appears late in the process and may reconstruct earlier generating structure retrospectively.

## Global structure versus local resolution

The disciplined form of the hypothesis is not that an original form literally sees the future. It is that the global mathematical structure is already determined and can contain relations beyond any local stage of a hypothetical unfolding.

## Non-identical return

The new recursive hypothesis is:

```text
cycle_n -> reintegration -> residual difference -> cycle_n+1
```

The residual difference may prevent perfect repetition and open a new scale.

This is currently a metaphysical hypothesis, not a theorem about pi.
