# Authors and Convergences

Historical parallels help locate Pi Theory. They do not prove it.

## Source and return

- Empedocles: integration and separation.
- Plotinus: procession from the One and return.
- Proclus: remaining, procession, reversion.
- Pseudo-Dionysius: procession from and return to the Good.
- Nicholas of Cusa: infinity, mathematical images, absolute and contracted reality.
- Jacob Boehme: manifestation through opposition and differentiation.

## Process and individuation

- Whitehead: the many become one through concrescence.
- Simondon: metastability, transduction, individuation.
- Teilhard: complexity, consciousness, and convergence.

## Language and symbolic cognition

- Plato: inspiration, recollection, cosmic order, and naming.
- Peirce: semiotics.
- Cassirer: symbolic forms.
- Schelling: conscious and unconscious production in art.
- Jung and Pauli: speculative psychophysical-ordering parallels, used cautiously.
