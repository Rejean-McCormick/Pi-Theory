# Matter, Life, and Consciousness

Pi Theory requires explicit mechanisms between these domains.

## Matter

The weakest current formulation is that physical structures can retain history through constraints, path dependence, and state memory.

```text
dynamics -> stable modes -> persistent physical structure
```

## Life

Living organization requires more than stable matter. Relevant mechanisms include energy flow, autocatalysis, compartmentation, self-maintenance, heredity, and evolution.

## Consciousness

The theory distinguishes regulation, cognition, internal models, reflexivity, and phenomenal consciousness. Integration alone is not sufficient.

## Reflexive loop

The broad hypothesis is:

```text
Source -> cosmos -> matter -> life -> consciousness
       -> symbolic representation -> recognition of structure
```

The observer is therefore not outside the system being studied.
