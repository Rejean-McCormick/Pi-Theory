# Language, Culture, and Attunement

## What the hypothesis does not say

Pi Theory does not claim that French, English, or any human language literally existed inside pi before humans.

## Shared-constraint hypothesis

The current idea is:

```text
one deeper structure
-> world
-> organisms
-> cognition
-> language and culture
```

Human languages may converge on recurring semantic or phonetic patterns because human bodies and minds are themselves products of the world they describe.

## Ordinary mechanisms matter

Infant articulation, onomatopoeia, historical inheritance, and cultural transmission can explain many recurring forms. They must be modeled rather than ignored.

## Scientific version

A testable attunement hypothesis should make preregistered cross-language predictions that outperform null models based on known phonology, embodiment, and linguistic history.
